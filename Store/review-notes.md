# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

HOW TO USE: A new install shows a welcome card. Tap "Add your first kid", type a name, pick an avatar, and Save. Open Grown-ups, then Chore ideas by age, tick a few chores and Add. The Today tab now shows big chore tiles; tap one to mark it done (confetti, stars). Bank shows the spend/save/give jars and the savings goal, Shop is the reward shop priced in stars, and Week shows the week grid with a printable chart.

PARENT PIN: A new install has no PIN, so Grown-ups opens directly. To test Kids mode, open Grown-ups, then Settings, then Set a PIN (any 4 digits, typed twice). With a PIN set, Grown-ups and the money buttons ask for it, and "Grown-up OK" can be switched on so ticks wait for approval. The sample family used in our screenshots and recording uses PIN 1234.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. There is one non-consumable purchase, Chores Pro (com.mattbusel.chores.pro). Free: up to two kids with every feature, including chores, stars, jars, allowance, approvals, the week grid, printable charts and the reward shop with its eight starter rewards (which can be renamed and repriced). Pro: a third and further kid, adding your own new rewards, and sharing the weekly summary. The purchase is only ever shown inside Grown-ups, which sits behind the parent PIN once one is set; the kids' screens (Today, Week, Bank, Shop) never show a price or a buy button. To see the paywall: with two kids added, open Grown-ups, then Kids, then Add a kid; or Grown-ups, Rewards, New reward; or the share button on "This week so far" in Grown-ups; or Grown-ups, Settings, See Pro. "Restore purchase" is on the paywall and in Grown-ups, Settings, under Chores Pro.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored in the app's Documents folder on the device as a JSON file. The optional daily reminder is a local notification; permission is asked only when the parent switches it on. No network requests are made.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Chores is a family chore chart and allowance tracker used by parents together with their children: kids tick chores and earn stars and pocket money, parents approve, pay allowance and set rewards. It is aimed at parents (it is not in the Kids category) and is rated 4+. There are no ads, no links out and no data collection. The one in-app purchase is only offered in the parent area (see IN-APP PURCHASE).

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials. Add a kid and some chores as described above. There is no default PIN on a new install.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. No network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. Built with SwiftUI, Foundation, UserNotifications (local reminder only) and UIKit (haptics, PDF rendering for the printable chart).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The money sign is a setting ($, £, €, A$, C$, ₹).

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app does not move real money; the "bank" is a record the parent keeps of pocket money. All art, text and code are my own work.
